## Location

`database/schema.sql`

## Failure scenario

Migrations 096-098 add overpayment_payee_id, interest_booking_mode, and recurring_extra_mode, but this PR does not update database/schema.sql as required. A fresh installation initialized from schema.sql will lack these columns; TypeORM queries loading Account or LoanScenario will then fail with undefined-column errors.

## Recommended fix

Mirror all three migrations, including the payee foreign key and index, in database/schema.sql.
