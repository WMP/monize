## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-history.ts:123`

## Failure scenario

The fallback lower bound is the first loan-account transaction. For an imported mortgage with an opening balance, no paymentStartDate, standalone interest expenses in January and February, and its first principal repayment in March, both earlier interest expenses are filtered out even though the new orphan-interest path is intended to represent interest-only grace periods.

## Recommended fix

Use an actual origination/opening date when available; when none is known, do not infer the lower bound from the first principal transaction in a way that excludes preceding interest expenses.
