## Location

`backend/src/loan-rate-changes/rate-change-inference.service.ts:241`

## Failure scenario

For every non-Canadian loan, the code multiplies a periodic rate by 365/days even though the repository's loan and non-Canadian mortgage formulas use a fixed periods-per-year rate. For a 5% monthly loan, interest calculated at 5%/12 yields inferred rates of about 5.43% over a 28-day gap and 4.91% over a 31-day gap. Detection can therefore report a wrong rate or mistake month-length changes for rate changes.

## Recommended fix

Invert the configured periodic-rate formula: use periodsPerYear for ordinary loans and non-Canadian mortgages, retaining the Canadian fixed-rate compounding conversion where applicable.
