## Location

`backend/src/accounts/loan-payment-detector.service.ts:149`

## Failure scenario

pairSeparateInterest fills interestAmount but leaves PaymentRecord.amount equal to the principal transfer. In SEPARATE mode, three $450 principal transfers with separate $50 interest expenses therefore produce detected paymentAmount=$450, although the installment is $500. Applying that detection seeds amortization with an insufficient payment.

## Recommended fix

When producing the detected contractual payment for separately booked interest, add the paired interest to the principal transfer or avoid proposing a payment amount when the full installment cannot be established.

## Re-review of `c8dfcf42a86b`

Not fixed. In SEPARATE mode, an unmatched payment is returned unchanged and later treated as a complete installment. This occurs with partially imported, uncategorized, or otherwise unpaired interest history.


## Re-review of `5587e4d3d4f1`

Not fixed. `detectRegularAmount` falls back to all records when every record has `interestUnmatched`; `pairSeparateInterest` also returns records unmarked when its interest query finds nothing. Thus, for a SEPARATE-mode loan with three $450 principal transfers whose separate $50 interest expenses are missing or uncategorized, the detector still returns paymentAmount=$450 even though the full $500 installment cannot be established. The new test covers partial pairing but not the all-unmatched case.
