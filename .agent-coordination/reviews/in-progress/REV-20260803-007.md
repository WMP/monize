## Location

`backend/src/accounts/loan-payment-detector.service.ts:149`

## Failure scenario

detectPaymentPattern unconditionally calls pairSeparateInterest. For an account configured as SPLIT, a payment missing its interest split can therefore acquire an unrelated standalone expense from the configured category and use it in estimatedInterestRate and projected split values, despite SPLIT explicitly meaning standalone expenses must not be considered.

## Recommended fix

Skip pairSeparateInterest when account.interestBookingMode is SPLIT, as RateChangeInferenceService already does.
