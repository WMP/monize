## Location

`frontend/src/components/accounts/loan-detail/PayoffComparisonChart.tsx:140`

## Failure scenario

projectionStartKey is taken from the next union point after history, but the union now also contains the full original contractual schedule. If history ends in January, the contractual curve has February through April, and the baseline projection begins in May, the chart marks February as Today and preserves February during sampling even though no projection value exists until May. With no history, it can mark the contractual origination month instead of the first projection month.

## Recommended fix

Find the first point containing baselineBalance or scenarioBalance after the historical endpoint and use that point for projectionStartKey and sampling preservation.
