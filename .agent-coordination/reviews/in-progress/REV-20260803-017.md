## Location

`frontend/src/components/accounts/loan-detail/ScheduleTableRow.tsx:134`

## Failure scenario

DisplayRow carries the rate change effective on the row's date, but the rate button always calls openAddWith. Clicking a projected installment whose date already has a saved rate change therefore attempts to create a second change for the same date and fails with the duplicate-date conflict instead of editing the existing row. The condition also makes historical single-payment rows editable despite the component's read-only contract for historical rates.

## Recommended fix

Use editing.openEdit(row.change) when change is present, use openAddWith only for projected rows without a change, and omit onEdit for historical rows.
