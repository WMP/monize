## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-past-impact.ts:139`

## Failure scenario

`timeline.startingPaymentAmount` is null when `rateChanges` is empty, so lines 151-160 always calculate a theoretical payment from the configured term and never consider `account.paymentAmount`. For a 0% $10,000 loan with a 60-month configured term and an actual contractual payment of $250, the baseline uses $166.6667 and ends after 60 payments instead of 40. After 12 ordinary $250 payments, a current projection ends at payment 40, causing Past Impact to report roughly 20 months saved even though no overpayment occurred.

## Recommended fix

Before falling back to the term-derived payment, use a validated stored or history-derived full installment when no usable installment exists in the rate timeline. Ensure it exceeds the original balance's first-period interest.
