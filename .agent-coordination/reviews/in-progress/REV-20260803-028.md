## Location

`backend/src/loan-rate-changes/loan-rate-changes.service.ts:455`

## Failure scenario

Only a transfer split whose memo contains the English substring "extra" is preserved. A valid recurring overpayment with memo "Additional principal", a localized memo, or another user-defined label is omitted when the payload replaces all splits. Updating or deleting a rate row can therefore remove that recurring overpayment and reduce future principal payments.

## Recommended fix

Preserve extra-principal splits structurally or by stable identifiers/configured recognition fields, not one English memo substring. Preserve all recognized extra splits and their metadata.

## Re-review of `16e52c3f757a`

Not fixed. The rebuilt extra split copies only transferAccountId, amount, and memo; it drops extraSplit.categoryId. For an account configured with overpaymentCategoryId="cat-overpayment" and a split categorized accordingly with memo "Additional principal", the first synchronization recognizes it but removes its category. On the next rate update or deletion, the split no longer matches the configured category or the literal "extra", so it is omitted. Additionally, splits.find preserves only one recognized extra-principal split if multiple exist.


## Re-review of `e7e3aa1d98e8`

Not fixed. createSplits infers any split with transferAccountId as TRANSFER and then forces its categoryId to null. For an extra-principal transfer recognized through overpaymentCategoryId="cat-overpayment" with memo "Additional principal", the first synchronization passes the category in its payload but persistence removes it. On the next synchronization, neither the configured category nor the literal "extra" matches, so the replacement payload omits and deletes the overpayment. The added two-sync test mocks scheduledTransactionsService.update and reuses its input rather than the persisted split, so it passes despite this failure.
