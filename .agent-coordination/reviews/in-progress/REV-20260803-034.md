## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-history.ts:263`

## Failure scenario

The orphan merge updates lastBalance only for emitted principal-payment events. For a loan that is at 800 after February's payment, draws another 500 in mid-February, and has an orphan interest charge in March, the March row displays 800 instead of the actual 1300 debt. Its reconstructed annual rate is consequently also wrong.

## Recommended fix

Re-walk the signed ledger, including draw transactions, when assigning balances to orphan interest events.
