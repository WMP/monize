## Location

`backend/src/loan-rate-changes/loan-rate-changes.service.ts:635`

## Failure scenario

The remaining amortization is clamped to at least 12 months. A mortgage with six months remaining therefore receives a payment calculated over twelve months, so it will retain a substantial balance at its configured payoff date instead of holding the remaining amortization constant.

## Recommended fix

Clamp to one valid payment period rather than twelve months, and reject effective dates beyond the configured amortization end.
