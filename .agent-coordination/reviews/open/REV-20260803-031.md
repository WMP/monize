## Location

`frontend/src/components/accounts/loan-detail/OverpaymentSimulator.tsx:213`

## Failure scenario

formatCurrency is called without currencyCode even though the simulator receives the loan's currency. For a EUR loan while the user's base currency is CAD, the hint displays the detected EUR amount as CAD while the adjacent input uses a euro prefix.

## Recommended fix

Call formatCurrency(detectedExtra, currencyCode), consistent with the other loan-detail amounts.
