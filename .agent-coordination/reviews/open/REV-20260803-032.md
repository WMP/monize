## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-history.ts:123`

## Failure scenario

The fallback lower bound is the first loan-account transaction. For an imported mortgage with an opening balance, no paymentStartDate, standalone interest expenses in January and February, and its first principal repayment in March, both earlier interest expenses are filtered out even though the new orphan-interest path is intended to represent interest-only grace periods.

## Recommended fix

Use an actual origination/opening date when available; when none is known, do not infer the lower bound from the first principal transaction in a way that excludes preceding interest expenses.

## Dispute rejected

When `account.paymentStartDate` is absent, `originationDate` becomes the date of the first loan-account transaction. For an imported mortgage with an opening balance, standalone January and February interest expenses, and its first principal repayment in March, `scopedInterestTransactions` rejects both interest expenses as preceding `originationDate`. They therefore never reach `pairSeparateInterestByDate` and cannot become the intended interest-only orphan rows. Nothing shown establishes that the first principal transaction is the loan's origination date or that excluding such grace-period interest is intended.


## Dispute rejected

The fix agent's own notes confirm the failure rather than rebut it. When `paymentStartDate` is absent, `originationDate` is set to the first loan-account transaction. If an imported mortgage has an opening balance, January and February standalone interest expenses, and its first principal repayment in March, the lower-bound filter removes both interest expenses before `pairSeparateInterestByDate` runs. They therefore cannot become orphan interest-only events. No repository evidence supplied here establishes that the first principal transaction is the origination date or that pre-repayment interest should be excluded.


## Dispute rejected

For an imported mortgage with an opening balance, January and February standalone interest expenses, and its first loan-account repayment in March, `scopedInterestTransactions` removes both expenses before `pairSeparateInterestByDate` can emit them as orphan interest-only rows. Nothing in the supplied code establishes that the first loan-account transaction is the origination date or makes this exclusion intentional.
