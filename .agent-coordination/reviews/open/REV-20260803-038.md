## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-history.ts:849`

## Failure scenario

The fetch is constrained only by source account and category, while lifetime scoping separates only non-overlapping loans. If a mortgage and car loan are active concurrently, both paid from the same chequing account and both using a general Loan Interest category, each loan fetches and attributes both loans' standalone interest expenses.

## Recommended fix

Require and apply a loan-specific discriminator or persisted association for standalone interest transactions; source account, category, and overlapping dates are insufficient.
