## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-schedule.ts:396`

## Failure scenario

lowerEnd uses numPayments even when the baseline did not pay off. For a loan requiring 12 payments but generated with maxPayments: 2, the baseline returns numPayments 2 and paidOff false. A LOWER_INSTALLMENT overpayment then re-levels the remaining balance to finish by payment 2, sharply increasing the installment instead of lowering it.

## Recommended fix

Use a baseline term only when baseline.paidOff is true; otherwise compute the actual payoff term with an adequate bound or report that a fixed end cannot be determined.
