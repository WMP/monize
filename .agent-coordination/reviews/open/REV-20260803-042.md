## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-schedule.ts:527`

## Failure scenario

Whenever fixedEndPeriod is set, every overpayment is followed by re-leveling to that fixed end, including SHORTEN_TERM events. At 0% on a 1200 balance with a 100 payment, fixedEndPeriod 12, and a 100 first-period SHORTEN_TERM lump sum, the code lowers subsequent payments and still takes 12 payments instead of keeping the 100 installment and paying off in 11. Similarly, a later LOWER_INSTALLMENT event targets the original lowerEnd and can restore term months previously removed by a SHORTEN_TERM event.

## Recommended fix

Track the target payoff period after each SHORTEN_TERM event and re-level only against the then-current end date; fixed-term re-leveling must not absorb principal explicitly marked to shorten the term.

## Re-review of `25df6e8cffd5`

Not fixed. `shortenApplied` causes `currentFixedEnd` to be recalculated from the balance after all overpayments, including LOWER_INSTALLMENT amounts from the same period. For example, at 0% with balance 2400, payment 100, fixedEndPeriod 24, and first-period lump sums of 200 SHORTEN_TERM plus 200 LOWER_INSTALLMENT, the shortened target should be period 22 and the lower-installment amount should reduce the remaining payment to about 90.48. Instead, the code calculates the target from the post-400 balance, sets it to period 20, and keeps payments at 100, so the LOWER_INSTALLMENT event also shortens the term.
