## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-schedule.ts:455`

## Failure scenario

Interest and principal for the current row are calculated before the fixed-term payment is re-leveled; re-leveling occurs only after the row. For a 100000 balance with 12 periods remaining whose rate rises from 0% to 12% on the first projected payment, that row still uses the old 8333.33 payment instead of the approximately 8884.88 payment required at the new rate, and later rows compensate with a different installment.

## Recommended fix

When fixedEndPeriod is active, calculate the term-preserving payment after applying the row's rate change but before calculating that row's principal and interest.
