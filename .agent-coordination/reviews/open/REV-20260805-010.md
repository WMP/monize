## Location

`backend/src/loan-rate-changes/loan-rate-changes.service.ts:463`

## Failure scenario

`buildScheduledUpdate` always replaces the linked schedule with a principal transfer plus a categorized interest split, without considering `account.interestBookingMode`. For a SEPARATE loan whose linked schedule is the principal transfer and whose interest is booked independently, any rate edit converts the principal schedule to SPLIT booking; an existing separate interest schedule then also remains and interest is booked twice.

## Recommended fix

Build the scheduled update according to the selected booking mode. In SEPARATE mode, update only the principal transfer and handle any separately linked interest schedule explicitly.
