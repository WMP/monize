## Location

`frontend/src/components/accounts/loan-detail/AmortizationScheduleTable.tsx:231`

## Failure scenario

Lines 230-232 add a gap row only when the first payment in a month has precededByGap. For a weekly loan with payments on Apr 24, May 1, May 8, and May 29, the gap detector correctly flags May 29 after the missing May 15 and May 22 installments. Because May 1 is the month's first row, the grouped May unit emits no gap warning at all.

## Recommended fix

Preserve gap markers from every child row when forming month groups, rendering the marker at the corresponding position or preventing grouping across a detected gap.
