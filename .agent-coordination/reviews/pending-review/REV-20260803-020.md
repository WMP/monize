## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-past-impact.ts:222`

## Failure scenario

The database stores money to four decimal places, but each overpayment is independently multiplied by 100 and rounded before summation. Two valid overpayments with principal `10.0050` are therefore counted as `10.01 + 10.01 = 20.02`, although their exact total is `20.0100`. Past Impact consequently overstates Extra Principal Paid by one cent.

## Recommended fix

Accumulate integer ten-thousandths with `Math.round(event.principal * 10000)` and divide the final sum by 10000; apply presentation rounding only after aggregation.
