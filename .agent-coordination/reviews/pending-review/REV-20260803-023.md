## Location

`backend/src/accounts/loan-payment-detector.service.ts:462`

## Failure scenario

After excluding transfers, the code applies Math.abs to every matching transaction without requiring an expense sign. A +$50 interest refund near a -$200 interest charge is therefore treated as $250 of interest rather than net $150 or excluded, inflating the detected rate.

## Recommended fix

Only accept transactions whose signed amount represents an expense, and handle refunds explicitly according to the ledger contract rather than converting every amount to an absolute value.
