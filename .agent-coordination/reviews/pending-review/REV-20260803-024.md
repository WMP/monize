## Location

`backend/src/loan-rate-changes/rate-change-inference.service.ts:87`

## Failure scenario

The inference query has no upper date bound. Future scheduled payments are included even though account.currentBalance excludes future-dated transactions. buildRunningBalanceMap then reverses those future payments from today's balance, inflating every earlier balance and understating inferred rates; future split payments can also become observations themselves.

## Recommended fix

Restrict inference transactions to non-void rows dated on or before todayYMD(), using the same request-timezone definition used for currentBalance.

## Re-review of `e956187a6e94`

Not fixed. `pairSeparateInterest` queries through `lastPaymentDate + 45 days`, even when that date is after today. `detectAndPersist` invokes this after filtering only the loan-account transactions. For example, with three historical principal payments where the first two have separately booked interest and the latest lacks it, a future scheduled interest expense from the same source account and category can fall within the pairing tolerance of the latest payment. It is then attached to that historical payment, supplies the third required observation, and allows an inferred rate to be persisted from data that currentBalance excludes.


## Re-review of `83e99d8b28ef`

Not fixed. `hasRealEvidenceNear` can validate a future-derived pairing using an unrelated earlier interest transaction. For example, with payments at today−60, today−30, and today, genuine interest expenses for the first two payments, and only a future expense at today+1 for the latest payment, `pairSeparateInterest` pairs the future expense to today's payment. The verification still returns true for today's payment because the genuine expense at today−30 is within its hard-coded 45-day window, so the future interest remains an observation and can supply the third observation needed to persist an inferred rate.
