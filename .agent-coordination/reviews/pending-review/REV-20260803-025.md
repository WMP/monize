## Location

`backend/src/loan-rate-changes/loan-rate-changes.service.ts:632`

## Failure scenario

recalculatePaymentForRate always uses account.currentBalance. For a change effective two years in the future, it calculates the new payment using today's higher balance but the shorter remaining term at the future date, overstating the payment. A past-dated change similarly uses today's lower balance instead of the balance that existed when the change took effect.

## Recommended fix

Derive the balance at effectiveDate from historical transactions for past dates or a projection for future dates. If that cannot be done reliably, permit automatic recalculation only for a change effective today and require an explicit payment otherwise.
