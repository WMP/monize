## Location

`backend/src/loan-rate-changes/loan-rate-changes.service.ts:461`

## Failure scenario

Principal is capped at the outstanding balance, but proposedPaymentAmount remains the full contractual payment. With a $100 balance and a $500 payment, the payload amount is about -$500 while its principal and interest splits total only about -$100.42. ScheduledTransactionsService rejects this mismatch, so confirmation fails or automatic sync silently leaves the old schedule in place.

## Recommended fix

After capping principal, derive the parent amount from actual interest plus capped regular principal plus any capped extra principal. Ensure combined principal and extra principal never exceed the remaining balance.
