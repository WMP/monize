## Location

`backend/src/loan-rate-changes/loan-rate-changes.service.ts:455`

## Failure scenario

Only a transfer split whose memo contains the English substring "extra" is preserved. A valid recurring overpayment with memo "Additional principal", a localized memo, or another user-defined label is omitted when the payload replaces all splits. Updating or deleting a rate row can therefore remove that recurring overpayment and reduce future principal payments.

## Recommended fix

Preserve extra-principal splits structurally or by stable identifiers/configured recognition fields, not one English memo substring. Preserve all recognized extra splits and their metadata.
