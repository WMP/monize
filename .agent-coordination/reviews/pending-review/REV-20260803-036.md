## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-history.ts:396`

## Failure scenario

Paired interest is rounded to cents here, and orphan interest is likewise rounded to cents before accumulation. A valid decimal(20,4) charge of 0.0049 becomes zero, while 0.0050 becomes 0.01; repeated entries therefore materially understate or overstate cumulative interest and the installment seeded from history.

## Recommended fix

Preserve four-decimal precision using integer arithmetic at 1/10000 units and round only presentation values to cents.
