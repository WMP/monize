## Location

`frontend/src/components/accounts/loan-detail/AmortizationScheduleTable.tsx:64`

## Failure scenario

For dates `2025-01-15`, `2025-02-15`, and `2025-08-15`, the intervals are 31 and 181 days. The code selects the upper value as the median, derives a 325.8-day threshold, and therefore fails to flag the six-month gap, despite claiming that three payments are sufficient.

## Recommended fix

For an even number of intervals, calculate the median from both middle values; also consider deduplicating payment dates before deriving the cadence.
