## Location

`database/migrations/098_loan_scenarios_recurring_extra_mode.sql:6`

## Failure scenario

The column is VARCHAR(16), but "LOWER_INSTALLMENT" is 17 characters. Creating or updating a scenario with recurringExtraMode="LOWER_INSTALLMENT" passes DTO validation and then fails with a PostgreSQL value-too-long error.

## Recommended fix

Increase recurring_extra_mode and the matching entity column to at least VARCHAR(17).
