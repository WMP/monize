## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-history.ts:132`

## Failure scenario

currentBalance was produced with Math.abs(), so a debt account with a +25 credit has currentBalance 25 and loanPaidOff becomes false. If that paid-off loan shares its source account and interest category with a later refinanced loan, later interest transactions are not bounded by the payoff date and appear as phantom rows on the old loan.

## Recommended fix

Determine payoff from the signed account balance, using debtMagnitude(Number(account.currentBalance)), rather than from its absolute value.
