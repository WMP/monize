## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-schedule.ts:580`

## Failure scenario

When no row is at or before the requested date, effectiveAnnualRateOn returns the earliest row rather than the fallback, and buildRateTimeline also adopts that row's payment. For a 5% loan with a 7%/$1200 change effective October 1 and a September payment, a timeline containing only that future row uses 7% and $1200 for September despite RateChange's first-payment-on-or-after contract.

## Recommended fix

Before the earliest effective date, retain fallbackAnnualRate and return no timeline payment; leave the future row solely in rateChanges until its effective date.
