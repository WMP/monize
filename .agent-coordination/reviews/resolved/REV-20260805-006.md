## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-past-impact.ts:109`

## Failure scenario

The function now returns null whenever both amortizationMonths and termMonths are absent, even though Account permits both to be null and a contractual schedule can be determined from the original principal, rate, frequency, and installment. For example, a $10,000 LOAN with a $500 monthly payment, 6% rate, paymentStartDate, and an initial rate-history row recording the $500 installment returns null solely because termMonths and amortizationMonths are null; the entire Past Impact section disappears instead of calculating the natural payoff schedule.

## Recommended fix

Require a configured term only for the PMT fallback. When a usable recorded or account contractual installment exists, generate its natural payoff schedule without fixedEndPeriod; make rescueEndPeriod conditional on a term being available.
