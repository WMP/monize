## Location

`frontend/src/lib/loan-past-impact.ts:192`

## Failure scenario

For ACCELERATED_BIWEEKLY and ACCELERATED_WEEKLY mortgages without a usable recorded installment, calculateMortgagePaymentAmount first derives the accelerated payment, but fixedEndPeriod then re-levels that payment on the first schedule row across the full nominal amortization. For a 25-year accelerated-biweekly mortgage, this replaces the monthly-payment/2 installment with the lower payment needed for 650 biweekly periods, stretching payoff back to 25 years. The resulting originalPayoffDate and totalInterest are wrong, which can also fabricate the reported months and interest saved.

## Recommended fix

Preserve the calculated accelerated installment and let it reach its natural earlier payoff. For accelerated frequencies, use rescueEndPeriod only to handle a later rate-driven stall and retain the normal high payment cap instead of fixedEndPeriod.
