# v1.18.0

The **banks, receipts and mortgages** release.

Monize can now **pull transactions straight from your bank** through Open Banking, and **read order confirmations from a mailbox** to fill in what each purchase was for. Mortgages gained a proper **type** (annuity, Canadian fixed, linear, interest-only), and a bank's monthly mortgage debit can now **settle the installment directly** instead of creating a duplicate. Transaction rules can split a row or turn it into a transfer, and a new **Cash Flow Sankey** report shows where your money came from and went.

## Bank sync (Open Banking)

**Settings -> Bank sync** connects a bank account through Open Banking and imports its booked transactions. Enable Banking is the first provider; its restricted production mode is free for personal use.

- **Guided setup.** The settings page walks you through creating your Enable Banking application, with every value to paste shown beside a copy button. Then pick your country and bank, authorize at the bank, and link each bank account to a Monize account. Matching is by account number, and "Create account" opens the account form already filled in.
- **Preview before import.** A newly linked account shows you every row it would import before anything is written. Untick a row to skip it this time or to exclude it for good. Each row shows its payee after mapping, the operation type as a tag, and which of your rules would fire and what they would change.
- **Sync now, or daily.** Rows arrive once, with your `import` rules applied. A bank with a built-in profile shows how its data behaves in the connect dialog.
- **Notifications** before a consent expires, and for each daily sync's outcome.

## Email receipts

A bank row says how much and when, not what you bought. **Settings -> Email receipts** connects a dedicated mailbox that receives order confirmations (Google and Microsoft 365 sign-in, or plain IMAP). Monize reads it, matches each email to the transaction that paid for it, and proposes a description, a payee and a split by line item with a category per line.

- Proposals arrive in the **AI review inbox**. Nothing changes until you approve, unless you turn on auto-apply.
- Built-in parsers for Amazon, Allegro, PayU and Google Play, a parser editor with a test panel, and **Draft parser with AI** from a few selected emails. **Recognize with AI** hands one email to the assistant in the chat.
- Forwarded emails are read by their original sender and date, so you can forward receipts from your main mailbox.
- **Tools -> Email Receipts** lists every email read, its state, and lets you link an unmatched one to a transaction by hand.
- A short **guided tour** explains why Monize reads receipts, why an AI provider is needed in practice, and where the profiles and the review inbox live. It is offered from the What's New digest and from **Settings -> Tours**.

## Mortgages

- **A Mortgage Type select** replaces the "Canadian Mortgage" and "Variable Rate" checkboxes on the mortgage form and in Set Up Payments. Choose **Annuity (Level Payment)** for a mortgage whose total payment is the same every period, including a variable-rate one, or **Canadian Fixed Rate** for a Canadian fixed-rate contract, whose interest compounds twice a year. Each option has a line of help saying how to recognise it.
- **Two new types: Linear and Interest Only.** **Linear (Constant Principal)** repays the same principal every period, so the payment falls as the balance does, the usual arrangement in the Netherlands and elsewhere in Europe. **Interest Only** charges only the interest each period, with the whole balance repaid at the end. For a linear mortgage, an extra repayment either shortens the term (the default) or lowers the installment; your lender's statement tells you which. These mortgages have no fixed payment: each installment is worked out on its due date from the balance and the rate, and the form, Loan Details, the simulator, the reports and the AI assistant all show the same dated figures.
- **Not sure which type you have?** When creating a mortgage, enter two or three consecutive installments from a statement and Monize suggests a type and says why. For an existing mortgage, **Detect Mortgage Type** in the Rate History panel of Loan Details reads your posted installments and suggests one.
- **Rate-Fixed Term for every mortgage.** The term used to appear only for Canadian mortgages. It is now offered for every mortgage and labelled as the period the rate is fixed for: a 5-year fixed rate, a UK fixed deal, a Dutch rentevaste periode, a German Zinsbindung. The reminder email before a term ends now says the rate term is ending rather than that a renewal is coming.
- **Payments are priced at their own due date.** A future-dated rate change recalculates the payment from the balance on the day it takes effect, and the scheduled bill steps to the new amount at the first installment on or after that date, not at whatever installment was next when you recorded it. Editing or deleting a rate change now asks before it changes the bill, and names the installment and date the new figures apply from. The Bills calendar, Upcoming Bills, the occurrence picker and "Edit occurrence" each show that occurrence's own payment and principal/interest split.
- **Scheduled loan payments are booked in cents** (or the currency's smallest unit), the way the bank debits them. Posting a linear mortgage's installment used to fail on a rounding mismatch.
- **"Payment from Account" can be changed** after a loan is created. Upcoming payments and the payment-matching rule move with it.
- The payoff chart on a mortgage with a past start date and no recorded payments now starts its projection in the right month, and the Today marker sits where it should.

### What changes for an existing mortgage

Each mortgage is given the type its checkboxes already meant: Canadian and not variable becomes Canadian Fixed Rate, every other combination Annuity. Until you record a rate change, the payment, the principal and interest split, the effective annual rate and the payoff projection are the same as before. The Variable Rate option used to say it "uses monthly compounding"; it never did, and that wrong description is gone.

**Known issue:** on a mortgage that had "Variable Rate" ticked and no rate history recorded in Loan Details, saving the account clears that flag, and the payment history then shows today's rate on past payments that charged no interest. Record the mortgage's rate changes in Loan Details to have each payment show the rate in effect on its date.

If you have a mortgage with more than one rate change recorded, its bill may still show a payment calculated the old way. Open Loan Details and re-save one of the rate changes; the scheduled payment updates.

## Loan settlement from bank debits

- **An imported bank debit can now settle a mortgage or loan installment directly.** When your bank posts one undivided monthly debit, Monize recognises that row, splits it into the principal and interest for the matched installment, books it as a transfer to the loan plus an interest line, and marks the scheduled bill occurrence as paid, instead of creating a second, duplicate transaction. This works for history you already imported, and for every future import, bank sync, or manually entered row.
- **"Payment matching"** on the mortgage form and in Set Up Payments: a payee pattern (prefilled with the institution name) and an optional description pattern. Saving creates a transaction rule with the new **Settle Loan Installment** action, visible under Tools -> Rules like any other, and turns off auto-posting on the linked bill so the bank's own row is what gets matched. You never type an amount; the rule prices each row itself, within a small tolerance for the bank's rounding.
- **Loan Details gains a Payment matching panel**: the linked rule, a **Process history** button that runs it over everything already imported, and a collapsible list of settled installments, each linking to its transaction.
- Nothing changes for a loan until you turn this on.

## Transaction rules

- **Test rule** in the editor's If section lists the existing transactions your conditions match, before you save.
- **Two new actions.** **Convert to transfer** turns a matched expense or income into a transfer to another account. **Split** divides a row into parts, with amounts taken from the text of the row (for example a bank line that reads `PRINCIPAL: 1200,50 INTEREST: 300,25`), each part a category or a transfer. Both are created through the AI assistant, and shown on the Rules page.
- **A date condition and an active window.** Rules can match on the transaction date, and a rule can be given a period outside which it never applies, so a reconciled history is not changed by saving or running a rule later.
- **Rule results in the import preview.** Expanding a row in the bank sync preview shows which rules match it and what they would change.
- A rule's **usage guide** has a loan example for the split and transfer actions.

## Reports

- **Cash Flow Sankey**, under Insights and as a dashboard widget: income categories flow into one Income node, and out to expense categories, savings and investments, debt payments and other accounts, with the leftover shown as "Unspent" or "Drawn from balances". Click any node to open its transactions. Phones open on the table view, and the diagram is sized for a phone screen.
- Portfolio Summary and Portfolio Value over Time **show TWR, MWR and Investment Result again** for imported investment history. In 1.17.0 these were withheld for nearly every QIF and CSV import, because a trade's cash leg on the brokerage was read as money leaving the portfolio.
- The Value Change, Investment Result and Portfolio Performance figures **update after a price refresh** without a page reload.

## Categories

- **Automatic amount sign can be turned off per category**, with subcategories inheriting the setting. Use it for a category that sometimes holds both directions.

## Accounts and transactions

- An **Edit Account** button in the account details header.
- The New Account and Edit Account form is wider.
- The +/- sign button in amount fields is drawn more clearly.

## Currencies

- **Small exchange rates are no longer stored rounded to nothing.** Yahoo returns a pair like IDR/SGD rounded to four decimals, which lost most of its digits. Monize now also asks the inverse pair and keeps whichever has more precision, and refuses to store a rate it cannot trust. Sparse rate histories are filled from the inverse pair too.
- **Exchange rates now come from an ordered chain of providers.** Frankfurter (the ECB's own reference rates: no key, no request cap, daily history back to 1999) is asked first, Yahoo Finance second, for every pair that is not one of a deployment's choosing. New rows for the 28 currencies the ECB publishes are now ECB reference rates dated by the ECB rather than Yahoo quotes; rates already stored are not rewritten. The Currencies page and the rate history dialog now say which provider a rate came from. Set `FX_PROVIDERS=yahoo,frankfurter` to ask Yahoo first instead.

## Notifications

- **Signing out no longer turns push off** for that browser. The device is held, receives nothing, and resumes when the same account signs in again. Settings shows a held device as such.

## Everyday fixes

- Opening the sign-in page with a session still valid no longer shows the form, and What's New no longer opens on top of it.
- The icon picker dropdown is no longer clipped inside a dialog, and the date shortcuts tooltip stays on screen.
- A restore interrupted by a backend restart can be retried within 15 minutes rather than an hour, and the message says how long to wait. A restore that ends with no answer from the server says to reload to see whether it applied.
- Garbled Korean, Vietnamese and Hindi translations were repaired.

## For operators

- **Bank sync** needs each user's own Enable Banking application credentials; nothing is configured server-side. Production Enable Banking applications need a privacy and terms URL, and `docs/legal/` has templates to publish. Raw bank data is only logged when `BANK_SYNC_LOG_RAW=true`.
- **Email receipts** adds a poll cron every 15 minutes, one poll per mailbox across replicas. A mailbox connection reaches only a public address unless the owner is an admin or the host is in `EMAIL_RECEIPTS_PRIVATE_HOST_ALLOWLIST`. Parsers are in backups; mailboxes and received emails are not.
- **The production image starts on CPUs without AVX** and on Alpine again; the PDF text extractor was swapped for one with no native binaries.
- The "client.query() when the client is already executing a query" Postgres warning and the "Unexpected token 'p'" boot warning in `CLUSTER_MODE=multi` are gone.
- Database migrations add the mortgage type (filled from the two existing checkbox columns), the linear prepayment setting, the loan settlement claim columns, the payment matching rule pointer, the rule active window, the category auto-sign setting, the push hold timestamp, and the bank sync and email receipts tables. All are additive. The mortgage checkbox columns are still written beside the type, so rolling back to 1.17.0 prices every mortgage as before; 1.17.0 reads a Linear or Interest Only mortgage as a level-payment mortgage with no stored payment.
- CSP headers now cover robots.txt and sitemap.xml, and preference cookies are marked Secure over HTTPS, closing the ZAP baseline findings.
