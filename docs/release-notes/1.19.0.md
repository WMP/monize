# v1.19.0

Government bonds, brokerage emails that become investment transactions, an advanced transaction search, hideable columns, and a Cash Flow Forecast on the dashboard.

## Government bonds

Monize now values government bonds from their terms of issue, not a market price. Each country needs its own adapter; **Poland** is the first, with all eight retail treasury bonds (OTS, ROR, DOR, TOS, COI, EDO, ROS, ROD).

- **Adding one:** choose Type **Bond**, Country **Poland** and the kind (for example EDO). Each purchase records its series; terms come from the official issue letters, and inflation (GUS) and the NBP reference rate are fetched for you.
- **Valuation:** a daily value per series with accrued interest and the early-redemption value. Figures are tested against the Ministry of Finance's published examples.
- **Redemptions** suggest the returned principal and the interest, net of any early-redemption fee, so interest is never booked as a capital gain.
- **Notifications:** a new **Bonds** category covers maturity in 14 days, interest paid, a new rate period, a matured series with no redemption, and a rate change from new inflation or NBP data.
- **Downloads only on request:** nothing is fetched until you add a bond, fetch a series or turn on automatic fetching.

Want your country's bonds? Open a Discussion naming the bond types and where their terms are published. `docs/backend/bond-adapters.md` shows what an adapter needs.

## Email receipts

### Brokerage trade confirmations

A profile can now read a broker's "order filled" email and propose the investment transaction, approved in the AI review inbox.

- **Buys, sells, dividends and reinvestments (DRIP)**, with symbol, shares, total or price, commission, date and account.
- **Accounts the broker prints alike** (a CAD and a USD RRSP) are told apart by currency.
- **Never booked twice:** a trade already in the ledger is not proposed again, and one that pays a scheduled investment transaction posts that occurrence.
- **Auto-apply is opt-in and strict:** the price must agree with the shares and the total. A complete cash dividend may auto-apply.

### Purchases paid later

For "buy now, pay later", a **purchase profile** stores the shop's itemised email and a **repayment profile** matches the bank transaction. When the repayment arrives, its transaction is split into every item of the purchases it pays, spread proportionally over instalments. A purchase email that arrives later updates the proposal. Joined proposals always wait for your approval.

### Working with many emails

- **Search** the list by text (subject, sender or body) and by date.
- **Import archived emails**, and page through long lists.
- **Act on a selection:** Reprocess, Restore ignored and Approve proposals, with **Deselect by** state, sender or date to narrow it.
- **Profiles:** patterns can span several lines, the order number goes in **Ref #**, and a profile can ignore or delete the emails it matches.
- **Fixed:** an order email no longer matches an income transaction of the same amount.

## AI review

- **Notifications:** a new **AI review** category tells you when proposals are waiting and when they expire in 3 days. Notices carry only counts and dates.
- **Send pending requests to the AI assistant** from the inbox, all or a selection, instead of waiting for an agent to claim them.
- **A rule whose only action is "request AI review"** can now be run on existing transactions.
- **Fixed:** "View email receipts" opens that email; a proposal from a connected MCP agent can be approved in the chat.

## Transactions

- **Advanced search:** `#10` finds cheque 10 only; `payee:costco -cat:gas`, `amount:50..100` and `date:2026-03` work too. A line under the box shows how the search was read, and **?** lists the syntax. **Change:** several words now match all of them anywhere; quote them (`"costco gas"`) for the exact phrase.
- **Hideable columns** on the register, the holdings table and the brokerage register, from the **Columns** menu or by long-pressing or right-clicking a header.
- **Default views:** Settings -> Preferences -> Default Views saves density, year display, chart folding, shown columns, and whether opening an account goes to its register or its dashboard. A browser's own choice still wins.
- **Collapsible chart** on the Transactions page.
- **Recent tags first** in the tag pickers.
- **Fixed:** a link to a transaction now reaches it when it is not on the first page.

## Dashboard and reports

- **Cash Flow Forecast widget**, the same forecast as Bills & Deposits, with its own period and accounts. Off by default; add it from Customize.
- **Overdue scheduled transactions count:** a bill that fell due and was neither posted nor skipped now lands on the first day of the Cash Flow Forecast and of the account balance forecasts, and the tooltip says so.
- **Net Worth Over Time** (report and widget): bars stacked by account type with a net worth line, or one solid bar, and a 10Y window. The widget gets a timeframe setting.
- **Top Recurring Expenses:** a 6M to All range, and the monthly estimate inside the donut.
- **Labels or Legend** view on Top Recurring Expenses, Expenses by Category and Assets vs Liabilities. Net worth now sits inside the Assets vs Liabilities donut.
- **Asset allocation by class** merges "Stocks" and "Equity" into one slice and translates class names.
- **Report links** to the register open the exact payee, so "Costco" no longer also matches "Costco Gas Bar".

## Investments, import and currencies

- **Reinvested distributions count as income** in the investment report, dividend reports and the security page. Voided rows no longer count, and dividend figures are converted from the row's own currency.
- **Turn off price updates** for a single security; turning them back on fetches the missing prices.
- **OFX re-imports skip rows already imported**, keyed by the bank's transaction id, and the result screen lists what was skipped.
- **Drag and drop** files onto the Import page.
- **Exchange rates come from Frankfurter first** (the ECB's reference rates, history back to 1999), then Yahoo. The Currencies page names the source of each rate.
- **Loans and mortgages** now require an interest category, and changing it moves the payment's interest line with it.
- The email receipts **guided tour** is in Settings -> Tours.

## For operators

- **13 migrations**, all additive (bonds, email receipt trades and links, view defaults, hidden columns, `transactions.external_id`, FX coverage providers). One data fix clears `skip_price_updates` on securities that carry a provider override, so they keep refreshing.
- **`FX_PROVIDERS`** defaults to `frankfurter,yahoo`; set `yahoo,frankfurter` for the old order. **`FX_FRANKFURTER_BASE_URL`** points at a self-hosted Frankfurter. Stored rates are not rewritten.
- **API:** `POST /transactions/bulk` creates up to 200 rows per call, and an optional `externalId` makes a retried create return the existing row.
- **Claude models** that reject sampling parameters (Opus 4.7+, the Claude 5 family) now work.
- Bond data is fetched from obligacjeskarbowe.pl, GUS and NBP, and only after a user adds a bond or turns on fetching.
